Reported Speech

Statements & Questions — Shifting from Present to Past Tense

📖

Introduction

When we report what someone said, we use reported speech (indirect speech). We do not always repeat the exact words — the verb tense shifts one step back into the past.

🗣️ Direct: "I am hungry."

📝 Reported: She said (that) she was hungry.

  • Use said or told + object to introduce the report.
  • The word that is optional but common.
  • Pronouns usually change to match the new speaker's point of view.
📢

Reported Statements

A statement is a declarative sentence. To report it:

  • Use said / told (someone)
  • Optionally add that
  • Shift the verb tense one step back
  • Adjust pronouns as needed
Pattern: Subject + said / told (sb) + (that) + subject + past verb…
Direct
"I love music."
Reported
She said she loved music.
Direct
"We are studying English."
Reported
They said they were studying English.
❓

Reported Questions

Reported questions use asked, have normal word order (no inversion), and remove auxiliary do/does/did.

Yes/No: Subject + asked + if / whether + subject + past verb

Wh-: Subject + asked + wh-word + subject + past verb

Yes/No Direct
"Do you have a pen?"
Reported
She asked if I had a pen.
Wh- Direct
"Where do you live?"
Reported
He asked where I lived.
Be Direct
"Are you tired?"
Reported
She asked if I was tired.

📄 The Interview

Yesterday, a journalist interviewed two students about their school life. The first student, Lucia, said that she loved her English class because the teacher was very creative. She also mentioned that she was preparing a project about endangered animals.

The second student, Marco, told the journalist that he could play the guitar and that he would perform at the school concert next month. When the journalist asked if he was nervous, Marco replied that he was a little nervous but very excited. He added that his classmates were very supportive.

At the end of the interview, Lucia asked the journalist what his favourite subject had been at school. The journalist laughed and said it was Science.
